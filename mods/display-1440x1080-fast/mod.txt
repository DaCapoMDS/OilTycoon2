1440x1080 with reflections and shadows off - the measured fix for 11 fps

Frame 33 ms and zero queue syncs, against 90-106 ms and 41 syncs with the
stock settings. Roughly 30 fps instead of 11.

Reflections are the culprit. The engine renders the scene again per
reflective surface and waits on each result, so the CPU stalls on the GPU
around 41 times a frame - which is why both sat idle while 90 ms elapsed, and
why a fast GPU and DXVK made no difference. Turning reflections off removes
every one of those stalls.

Shadows do not stall but cost roughly 26 ms of straight render time, so they
go too.

Everything else stays: trees, water, particles, cars, waves, full texture
detail, full draw distance. The view still looks like the game.

Resolution is 4:3 at full screen height, because the engine has no
aspect-ratio correction - see docs/TUNING.md.
