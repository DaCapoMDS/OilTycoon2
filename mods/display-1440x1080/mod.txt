4:3 at 1440x1080 - correct proportions, roughly twice the pixels of the default

The engine has no aspect-ratio correction, so a 16:9 backbuffer stretches the
image horizontally by 1.333x instead of widening the view. 1440x1080 is 4:3,
uses the full height of a 1080p panel, and is the largest undistorted option.

Windowed, because fullscreen validates the resolution against an enumerated
mode list and silently falls back to 1280x1024.

Note that the game rewrites DATA/ot2.cfg itself on exit. Applying this mod
seeds the values; the game then maintains them.
