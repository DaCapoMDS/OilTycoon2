DXVK - translates the game's Direct3D 9 calls to Vulkan

For the 20 fps problem. Engines of this era issue thousands of small draw
calls per frame, and modern drivers carry far more per-call overhead on the
legacy D3D9 path than 2006 drivers did, so the bottleneck is the CPU feeding
the GPU rather than the GPU itself - which is why a fast card does not help
and why the frame rate barely moves with resolution.

DXVK collapses that overhead. It cannot affect game speed: the simulation
runs on a logical clock, not on frames.

This drops a d3d9.dll next to game.exe, which Windows loads in preference to
the system one. Reverting deletes it and the game goes straight back to the
system D3D9.

Not part of this repository. DXVK is third-party (zlib licence,
github.com/doitsujin/dxvk) and its binary is not redistributed here -
tools\get-dxvk.ps1 fetches it. Build used here: v3.1.1, x32, because game.exe
is a 32-bit binary.
