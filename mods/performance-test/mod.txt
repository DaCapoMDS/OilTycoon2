Diagnostic: every expensive pass turned off, same resolution

Not meant as a way to play - it is an A/B against the normal settings, to find
out whether 20 fps in a round is the renderer or the simulation.

Reflections and shadows each re-render part of the scene, and citydistance
drives how many objects get drawn at all. If the frame rate barely moves with
all of this off, the cost is not in rendering and no graphics setting will fix
it - look at Logic Time in the F1 overlay instead.
